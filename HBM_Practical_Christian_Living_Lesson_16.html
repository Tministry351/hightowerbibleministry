Lesson 16

Growing into Spiritual Maturity

Primary Scripture: 2 Peter 3:18

Supporting Scriptures: John 17:3; John 15:5; Galatians 5:22–23; 2 Timothy 2:2; Galatians 6:1–2; Psalm 139:23–24; 1 John 1:9

Memory Verse: 2 Peter 3:18

Central Teaching Truth

Spiritual maturity is a lifelong process of growing in the grace and knowledge of Jesus Christ, becoming more like Him in character, truth, love, discernment, and obedience.

Lesson Purpose

This final lesson helps believers understand that Christian maturity is not simply knowing more information. It is an ongoing life of knowing Christ, abiding in Him, producing spiritual fruit, restoring fellowship when we fail, and helping others grow.

1 | Observation — What Does the Scripture Say?

Read 2 Peter 3:18.

“But grow in grace, and in the knowledge of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ. To him be glory both now and for ever. Amen.” — 2 Peter 3:18

Peter commands believers to grow in two connected areas: grace and the knowledge of Jesus Christ. Christian life involves continued development rather than remaining spiritually unchanged.

2 | Interpretation — What Is Spiritual Maturity?

Spiritual maturity is the ongoing process of becoming more like Christ in character, discernment, love, and obedience through God's grace and the work of the Holy Spirit.

Maturity does not mean sinless perfection. It means that the direction of our lives increasingly reflects Christ.

3 | Knowing Christ, Not Merely Following Religious Routines

Jesus describes eternal life in terms of knowing the Father and the Son in John 17:3.

Reading Scripture, praying, worshiping, and serving are important spiritual practices, but their purpose is to deepen our relationship with God, not merely to complete religious activities.

Christian maturity grows from relationship with Jesus Christ.

4 | Abiding in Christ Produces Fruit

“I am the vine, ye are the branches ... for without me ye can do nothing.” — John 15:5

Branches bear fruit only through their connection to the vine. In the same way, believers grow spiritually as they remain dependent upon Christ.

Galatians 5:22–23 describes the fruit produced by the Spirit: love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control.

5 | How Can Spiritual Growth Become Visible?

  • Character: increasing humility, patience, forgiveness, and love toward others.
  • Discernment: examining teaching carefully by the Word of God.
  • Obedience: applying Scripture rather than merely hearing it.
  • Prayer: growing in dependence upon God instead of self-sufficiency.
  • Service: using our gifts to strengthen others rather than simply seeking personal benefit.
  • Love: increasingly reflecting the character of Jesus Christ in relationships.

6 | Growing Believers Encourage and Disciple Others

Paul instructed Timothy to entrust sound teaching to faithful people who would be able to teach others also in 2 Timothy 2:2.

Spiritual maturity is not only personal growth. Mature believers help others understand Scripture, develop healthy Christian habits, and grow in faith.

Discipleship includes teaching, encouragement, correction, prayer, example, and patient relationship.

7 | When Growth Stalls — Repentance, Fellowship, and Restoration

A Christian who has drifted should not hide in shame or isolate from all support.

Galatians 6:1–2 calls mature believers to restore others with gentleness while also guarding themselves from temptation.

  • Recognize: ask God to search the heart and reveal wrong directions (Psalm 139:23–24).
  • Repent: confess sin, turn from it, and trust God's forgiveness (1 John 1:9).
  • Reconnect: return to prayer, Scripture, worship, and fellowship with other believers.
  • Receive help: allow spiritually mature believers to encourage, correct, and walk with you.

8 | Practical Application — A Next Step This Week

Choose one area where you need to grow. Read a related passage each day, pray honestly about it, take one concrete step of obedience, and speak with a mature believer who can encourage you.

Spiritual growth usually happens through faithful, repeated obedience rather than sudden dramatic change.

9 | Reflection and Discussion

  1. What is the difference between knowing about Christ and knowing Him personally?
  2. What evidence of the Spirit's fruit do you see developing in your life?
  3. In what area of character do you most need to grow?
  4. How does abiding in Christ affect spiritual maturity?
  5. Who has helped disciple or encourage you spiritually?
  6. Is there someone you could begin encouraging or discipling?
  7. What should a believer do when spiritual growth has stalled?

10 | Closing Prayer

Heavenly Father, thank You for calling us to know You through Your Son, Jesus Christ.

Help us grow in grace and truth. Forgive us when we drift, restore our fellowship with You, and lead us by Your Holy Spirit.

Produce the character of Christ in us. Give us wisdom to understand Your Word, courage to obey it, and love for others that leads us to encourage and disciple them faithfully. In Jesus' name, amen.
