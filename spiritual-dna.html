The principles that guide our ministry

Our Spiritual DNA

The ten biblical commitments that shape how Hightower Bible Ministry teaches, serves, and lives God's Word.

The Spiritual DNA Principles of Hightower Bible Ministry
These ten principles are presented with the complete teaching content from the ministry's original document.

Principle 1. God’s Glory is our Highest Purpose

We play a very important role in God's Kingdom as the Apostle Paul says in 1 Corinthians 10:31, “whatever you do, do all to the glory of God”. Also, in Ephesians 5:27, that He (Christ) might present her to Himself a glorious church, not having spot or wrinkle or any such thing, but that she should be holy and without blemish. Glorifying God is our highest priority.

Principle 2. God's Word Is Our Final Authority

We read in 2 Timothy 3:16-17, “All Scripture is breathed out by God and profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for training in righteousness, that the man of God may be complete, equipped for every good work” . God is our ultimate authority who gives us the Scriptures so they would be the final authority over our lives.

Principle 3. Jesus Christ Is the Center of Our Message

Following Jesus is the only way to receive salvation. So, what does the Bible say, Romans 10:9, “if you confess with your mouth that Jesus is Lord and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved ”. Also, what did God do, in John 3:16, "For God so loved the world, that he gave his only Son, that whoever believes in Him should not perish but have eternal life”. These two scriptures in the Bible, give this Ministry the foundation to stand on. John 14:6 Jesus said to him, "I am the way, and the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me”. Acts 4:12, we read “there is salvation in no one else, for there is no other name under heaven given among men by which we must be saved." “As we follow Christ and obey His Word, we trust in His promise to be with us always.” God will use Hightower Bible Ministry as a tool and resource for accurately preaching and teaching the word of God.

Principle 4. The Holy Spirit Empowers Our Ministry

We teach the Holy Spirit is God the third person of the Trinity. Scriptures confirm the nature of the Holy Spirit. When Peter said, in Acts 5:3-4, “Ananias, why has Satan filled your heart to lie to the Holy Spirit and to keep back for yourself part of the proceeds of the land? While it remained unsold, did it not remain your own? And after it was sold, was it not at your disposal? Why is it that you have contrived this deed in your heart? You have not lied to man but to God”, Here we see the Holy Spirit can be lied to. In Ephesians 4:30, “ And do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God, by whom you were sealed for the day of redemption”. “The Holy Spirit can grieve, demonstrating that He is a divine Person and not merely an impersonal force or power.” The Holy Spirit helps us to pray, “He convicts the world concerning sin, righteousness, and judgment, and He guides believers into truth”. He gives us directions to follow the way of God and not the way of the world.

Principle 5. Our Mission is The Gospel

The good news of Jesus Christ and what God has accomplished through the death, burial, and resurrection of His Son for sinners. God loves us so much that He sent His Son to suffer the wrath for our sins. The Apostle Paul said 1 Corinthians 15:3-4, “For I delivered to you first of all that which I also received: that Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures, and that He was buried, and that He rose again the third day according to the Scriptures”. Jesus's resurrection verifies the fact He paid the full price for our sins. So, the good news is Christ is our risen Savior.

Principle 6. Discipleship is Our Calling

Every follower of Jesus is called to follow Him faithfully and participate in making disciples. Matthew 28:19-20 , “Go therefore and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all things that I have commanded you; and lo, I am with you always, even to the end of the age." In Matthew 28:18 , "All authority has been given to Me in heaven and on earth”. Because all authority in heaven and on earth belongs to Jesus Christ, He commissions His followers to go and make disciples of all nations.”

Principle 7. Truth must be lived

John 14:6 , Jesus said "I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through Me”. Jesus didn't just explain the truth; He is the Truth. We walk by faith and live out the truth by modeling the decision-making people of God. James 1:22, “Be doers of the word, and not hearers only, deceiving yourselves”. Our hearts must remain receptive and teachable, continually receiving and applying God's Word.

Principle 8. The Church is God's Design

We are the body of Christ who exist for the equipping of the saints for the work of ministry, for the edifying of the body of Christ, Ephesians 4:12 . 1 Corinthians 12:12, For as the body is one and has many members, but all the members of that one body, being many, are one body, so also is Christ . God uses the human body to illustrate the need for unity and diversity that exists in the church. God uses the Church as His instrument to proclaim the Gospel, stand for truth, and resist the spiritual forces of evil.

Principle 9. Grace and Truth Guide our Ministry

John 1:14 , “And the Word became flesh and dwelt among us, and we beheld His glory, the glory as of the Only Begotten of the Father, full of grace and truth”. Grace isn't something God gives after we realized we don't deserve it. Grace is God's unmerited favor toward undeserving sinners, ultimately displayed in Jesus Christ. “ John 1:17 reminds us that grace and truth came through Jesus Christ.”. And the best is Ephesians 2:8-9 , “For by grace you have been saved through faith, and that not of yourselves; it is the gift of God, not of works, lest anyone should boast”. Our ministry is committed to proclaiming the truth of Jesus Christ so that people may understand and respond by faith to the saving grace God freely offers through Him .

Principle 10. Christ’s Return Gives us Hope

We believe heaven is our hope and God's plan. 1 Thessalonians 4:16-17 , “For the Lord Himself will descend from heaven with a shout, with the voice of an archangel, and with the trumpet of God. And the dead in Christ will rise first. Then we who are alive and remain shall be caught up together with them in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air. And thus, we shall always be with the Lord”. 1 John 3:2-3 , “ Beloved, now we are children of God; and it has not yet been revealed what we shall be, but we know that when He is revealed, we shall be like Him, for we shall see Him as He is, and everyone who has this hope in Him purifies himself, just as He is pure”. With the anticipation of Christ’s return our hope motivates us to live our lives pure, set apart and devoted to God. Hope is essential to help us persevere through difficult times. Without hope we can give up, we believe our ultimate hope is Jesus Christ and His promised return.
