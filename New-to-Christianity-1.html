Lesson 1: What Does It Mean to Be a Christian?

2 Corinthians 5:17 (KJV)

"Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new."

Welcome! Your Journey Can Begin Here

Have you ever wondered what it really means to be a Christian?

Perhaps you've attended church, heard about Jesus Christ, or grown up in a religious family. But you may still have questions about what it means to know God personally.

Becoming a Christian is not simply about joining a religion or attending church. It is about coming to know God personally through faith in His Son, Jesus Christ, receiving His forgiveness, and beginning a new life with Him.

1. Who Is Jesus Christ?

Jesus Christ is the Son of God who came into the world to save humanity from sin.

He lived a sinless life, died on the cross for our sins, and rose from the dead.

Jesus said in John 14:6:

"I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me."

What this means for you: Christianity begins with who Jesus is and what He has done, not how religious you have been.

2. Why Do We Need Forgiveness?

Romans 3:23 tells us:

"For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God."

Sin includes thoughts, attitudes, words, and actions that go against God's will.

Everyone has sinned. No one can earn forgiveness simply by doing good things.

Yet God loves us and provided a way for us to be forgiven through Jesus Christ.

Read also: Romans 5:8.

3. How Does Someone Become a Christian?

Salvation is God's gift of grace, received through faith in Jesus Christ.

Grace means God's undeserved kindness and favor. Salvation cannot be earned through good works or religious activities.

The Bible calls us to repent, which means turning away from sin and turning toward God, and to trust Jesus Christ as Savior and Lord.

Jesus died for our sins and rose again. Our hope rests in what He has done.

Read: Ephesians 2:8-10; Romans 10:9-10; Acts 3:19.

4. What Does It Mean to Be Born Again?

Jesus told Nicodemus in John 3:3:

"Except a man be born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God."

Jesus was speaking about a new spiritual birth, not being born physically a second time.

When someone receives new life through faith in Christ, the Holy Spirit begins changing that person's heart, attitudes, and desires.

Read also: John 3:5-8; Titus 3:5.

5. What Happens After Becoming a Christian?

Becoming a Christian is the beginning of a lifelong journey of following Jesus.

We grow through prayer, reading God's Word, learning to obey Christ, and spending time with other believers.

Christians still face challenges and sometimes make mistakes. But God continues His work in those who belong to Him.

Read: Philippians 1:6; 2 Peter 3:18.

Something to Think About

Have I been depending on church attendance, good works, or religious traditions to make me right with God?

Or have I personally placed my trust in Jesus Christ as my Savior and Lord?

Take time to consider what God's Word teaches and what it means for your life.

Your Next Step: A Personal Invitation

Perhaps you are beginning to understand what it means to be a Christian.

Maybe you are ready to place your faith in Jesus Christ, or perhaps you still have questions and need time to think.

Jesus said in John 6:37:

"...him that cometh to me I will in no wise cast out."

You can turn to God in repentance and place your trust in Jesus Christ.

You can speak to God in your own words. No special prayer earns salvation. Salvation is God's gift, received through faith in Jesus Christ.

If you are not ready, continue learning, asking questions, and reading Scripture.
