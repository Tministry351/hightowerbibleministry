What Does Spiritual DNA Mean?

The Spiritual DNA of Hightower Bible Ministry represents the biblical convictions that define who we are and guide everything we do.

Our mission explains why we exist. Our vision describes what we prayerfully seek to accomplish. Our Statement of Faith declares what we believe. Our Ministry Philosophy explains how we carry out our calling. Our Spiritual DNA reveals who we are.

These convictions are rooted in Scripture, centered upon Jesus Christ, and dependent upon the work of the Holy Spirit.

The Ten Spiritual DNA Principles

Principle 1

God's Glory Is Our Highest Purpose

Everything we do ultimately exists for the glory of God.

Primary Scriptures:
1 Corinthians 10:31 • Ephesians 5:27
Principle 2

God's Word Is Our Final Authority

God is our ultimate authority, and He has given us the Scriptures as the authoritative standard governing our doctrine, teaching, decisions, and lives.

Primary Scripture:
2 Timothy 3:16–17
Principle 3

Jesus Christ Is the Center of Our Message

Jesus Christ is not simply one subject among many. He is the center of our proclamation and the only way of salvation.

Primary Scriptures:
Romans 10:9 • John 3:16 • John 14:6 • Acts 4:12
Principle 4

The Holy Spirit Empowers Our Ministry

The Holy Spirit is the divine third Person of the Trinity. We depend upon Him to convict, guide, teach, empower, and transform God's people.

Primary Scriptures:
Acts 5:3–4 • Ephesians 4:30
Principle 5

Our Mission Is the Gospel

The Gospel is the good news of what God has accomplished for sinners through the death, burial, and resurrection of His Son, Jesus Christ.

Primary Scripture:
1 Corinthians 15:3–4
Principle 6

Discipleship Is Our Calling

Every follower of Jesus Christ is called to follow Him faithfully and participate in making disciples. Because all authority in heaven and earth belongs to Jesus Christ, He commissions His followers to go and make disciples of all nations.

Primary Scripture:
Matthew 28:18–20
Principle 7

Truth Must Be Lived

God's Word is not given merely to increase our knowledge. Biblical truth should transform how we think, make decisions, conduct ourselves, and live every day. Jesus didn't merely explain truth—He is the Truth.

Primary Scriptures:
John 14:6 • James 1:22
Principle 8

The Church Is God's Design

The Church is the Body of Christ. God has given believers different gifts and responsibilities while joining them together as one body. The Church exists to equip God's people, build up believers, proclaim the Gospel, stand for truth, and accomplish God's purposes in the world.

Primary Scriptures:
Ephesians 4:12 • 1 Corinthians 12:12
Principle 9

Grace and Truth Guide Our Ministry

Grace is God's unmerited favor toward undeserving sinners, ultimately displayed through Jesus Christ. We proclaim God's truth while extending the grace, compassion, and love demonstrated by Christ.

Primary Scriptures:
John 1:14 • John 1:17 • Ephesians 2:8–9
Principle 10

Christ's Return Gives Us Hope

Our ultimate hope is Jesus Christ and His promised return. The anticipation of Christ's return motivates us to live pure, holy, set-apart lives devoted to God. This hope also strengthens us to persevere through difficult circumstances.

Primary Scriptures:
1 Thessalonians 4:16–17 • 1 John 3:2–3

Who We Are

These principles are not simply statements for a website. They describe the biblical convictions Hightower Bible Ministry seeks to follow in its teaching, discipleship, ministry, and Christian witness.
