Lesson 3 — Jesus Christ

Part 1 — Who Is Jesus Christ?

Before we study what Jesus accomplished for our salvation, we must first understand who Scripture reveals Him to be.

Primary Scriptures

Primary Passages: John 1:1–18 • Colossians 1:15–20 • Philippians 2:5–11 • Hebrews 1:1–4

Supporting Scriptures: Matthew 16:13–17 • John 8:58 • John 20:28 • 1 Corinthians 8:6
Central Teaching Truth

Jesus Christ is the eternal Son of God, the Word who became flesh, the promised Messiah, the Creator and Lord of all, and the One who perfectly reveals the Father.

Introduction — The Question Every Person Must Answer

During His ministry, Jesus asked His disciples what people were saying about Him. Several answers were being given, but Jesus made the question personal: Who do you say that I am?

That question remains central to Christianity. Jesus cannot be understood merely as a wise teacher, moral example, prophet, or religious leader.

Scripture presents Him as the eternal Son, the promised Christ, the Word who became flesh, and the Lord through whom God created and sustains all things.

1. Observation — What Does Scripture Say?

Read Matthew 16:13–17, John 1:1–18, and Colossians 1:15–20. Observe what these passages actually say about Jesus before moving to interpretation.

2. Interpretation — What Does Scripture Mean?

A. Jesus Is the Eternal Word and Son

John begins his Gospel before Bethlehem. The Word already existed in the beginning. He was with God, and He shared the divine identity.

Jesus did not begin to exist when He was born in Bethlehem. The Son existed before His earthly life and entered human history according to God's saving purpose.

B. Jesus Reveals the Father

Scripture presents Jesus as the One who makes God known. John 1:18, Colossians 1:15, and Hebrews 1:3 emphasize that the Son uniquely reveals the character and glory of God.

To understand who God is, we must pay close attention to the person, words, and works of Jesus Christ.

C. Jesus Is Creator and Lord

John 1 and Colossians 1 place Jesus on the Creator side of the Creator-creature distinction.

All things came into being through Him, and creation continues to depend upon Him. Jesus is therefore not merely one important figure within creation. He is Lord over creation.

D. The Eternal Son Became Truly Human

John 1:14 teaches that the Word became flesh. Philippians 2 explains that Christ humbled Himself and entered the condition of human life.

Christianity does not teach that Jesus only appeared to be human. The eternal Son truly entered our world and lived among us.

E. Jesus Is the Promised Christ

The title Christ means Messiah or Anointed One. Peter's confession in Matthew 16 identifies Jesus as the promised Christ and the Son of the living God.

Jesus fulfills God's promises and stands at the center of God's plan of redemption.

Teaching Sentence: Jesus Christ is not merely a teacher who tells us about God; He is the eternal Son who entered human history to reveal God and accomplish His redemptive purpose.

3. Biblical Truth — What Must We Believe?

A biblical understanding of Jesus must begin with what Scripture says about Him, not with human opinion, religious tradition, or personal preference.

Scripture reveals Jesus as eternal, divine, truly human, Creator, Lord, Messiah, and Son of God.

These truths are not secondary details. Who Jesus is determines how we understand His words, His works, His death, His resurrection, and the salvation He offers.

Biblical Truth: Our faith must rest in the Jesus revealed in Scripture, not in a Jesus shaped by human opinion.

4. Application — How Should This Change My Life?

If Jesus is truly Lord, then He deserves more than admiration. He deserves our trust, worship, obedience, and allegiance.

The question Jesus asked His disciples is therefore personal: Who do I believe Jesus is, and does my life reflect that confession?

5. Personal Study and Discussion

6. Weekly Assignment and Memory Verse

This week: Read John 1:1–18 and Colossians 1:15–20 at least twice.

Make a list of every title, description, action, and claim connected with Jesus Christ. Then write one paragraph answering the question: Who is Jesus Christ?

Memory Verse: Matthew 16:16

Read the verse in your Bible, explain it in your own words, and practice memorizing it.

Closing Prayer

Father, help me to know Your Son as Scripture reveals Him. Guard me from reducing Jesus to human ideas or opinions. Give me understanding, faith, humility, and obedience as I learn more about who He is. Help my life and teaching honor Jesus Christ as Lord. Amen.
