About the Ministry

Welcome to Hightower Bible Ministry

Hightower Bible Ministry exists to glorify God by faithfully teaching His Word verse by verse, equipping believers and non-believers to grow in spiritual maturity, strengthening the local church, and proclaiming the Gospel of Jesus Christ with truth, grace, and conviction.

Our Purpose

Leading souls from the dominion of darkness, helping them live lives that glorify God, and giving them biblical tools that strengthen their Christian walk.

Learn God's Word

Discover what Scripture teaches through clear and understandable verse-by-verse Bible teaching.

Grow in Christ

Develop a stronger Christian walk through biblical truth and practical application.

Live God's Word

Learn how biblical truth applies to everyday life, marriage, family, relationships, and Christian service.
