What We Believe

Statement of Faith

The biblical beliefs that guide the teaching, ministry, and Christian witness of Hightower Bible Ministry.

Our Biblical Foundation

Hightower Bible Ministry seeks to build its beliefs and teaching upon the Word of God. The following statements summarize the biblical convictions that guide this ministry.

Statement 1

We Believe in One God — The Trinity

We believe in one God, who eternally exists in three Persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — one in divine nature and essence, yet distinct in Person.

Key Scriptures:
Matthew 28:19; 2 Corinthians 13:14
Statement 2

We Believe in God the Father

We believe in God the Father, the Almighty God, Creator and Sovereign LORD over all things.

Key Scriptures:
Genesis 1:1; Isaiah 46:9–10
Statement 3

We Believe in Jesus Christ

We believe in Jesus Christ, God's only begotten Son, sent by the Father to reveal God to mankind and redeem sinners.

Key Scriptures:
John 1:1–3, 14; John 3:16
Statement 4

We Believe in the Holy Spirit

We believe in the Holy Spirit, who proceeds from the Father and the Son and works in God's people. We depend upon Him to convict, guide, teach, empower, and transform God's people.

Key Scriptures:
John 14:16–17, 26; Acts 5:3–4; Ephesians 4:30
Statement 5

We Believe in the Holy Scriptures

We believe that the Bible is the inspired and authoritative Word of God, given through holy men moved by the Holy Spirit. Scripture is the authoritative standard for our doctrine, teaching, decisions, and lives.

Key Scriptures:
2 Timothy 3:16–17; 2 Peter 1:20–21
Statement 6

We Believe in Humanity

We believe that God created mankind, male and female, in His own image, uniquely designed to know Him, represent Him, live in relationship with Him and one another, and glorify Him.

Key Scriptures:
Genesis 1:27; Isaiah 43:7
Statement 7

We Believe in Sin and the Fall

We believe that through Adam's sin, sin and death entered the world and mankind became fallen and separated from fellowship with a holy God. All people are sinners by nature and by their own actions, fall short of the glory of God, and stand in need of God's forgiveness and salvation.

Key Scriptures:
Psalm 51:5; Isaiah 59:2; Romans 3:23; Romans 5:12; Romans 6:23
Statement 8

We Believe in Salvation

We believe that humanity cannot overcome the consequences of sin through good works, moral living, religious activity, or human effort. Every person needs Jesus Christ.

Salvation is God's gracious gift to sinners, made possible through the sacrificial death and bodily resurrection of Jesus Christ. Through Christ, God provides forgiveness of sins, righteousness, reconciliation with Himself, and eternal life.

Whoever calls upon the name of the Lord in repentance and faith, trusting in Jesus Christ for salvation, will be saved. Salvation is by God's grace through faith in Jesus Christ and not by our own works. All glory belongs to God.

Key Scriptures:
John 3:16; Romans 3:23–26; Romans 5:8–11; Romans 10:9–13; Ephesians 2:8–9; 1 Corinthians 15:3–4
Statement 9

We Believe in the Christian Life and Sanctification

We believe that being born again through faith in Jesus Christ is the beginning of the Christian life. Every believer is set apart for God and is called to live a life that honors Him rather than being conformed to the ways of this world.

Sanctification is the continuing work of God in the life of the believer through the Holy Spirit and the Word of God. Through this process, God transforms our thinking, character, desires, and conduct as we put off the old self, put on the new self, and grow increasingly into the likeness of Jesus Christ.

The Christian life is a life of continuing spiritual growth, obedience, repentance, faith, and dependence upon the Holy Spirit.

Key Scriptures:
John 3:3–7; Romans 8:29; Romans 12:1–2; 2 Corinthians 3:18; Galatians 5:16–25; Ephesians 4:22–24; Philippians 1:6; 1 Thessalonians 4:3
Statement 10

We Believe in Baptism and the Lord's Supper

We believe that baptism is an act of obedience for those who have placed their faith in Jesus Christ. As the believer goes under the water and emerges from it, baptism symbolizes the believer's identification with the death, burial, and resurrection of Jesus Christ.

Baptism does not earn salvation but publicly demonstrates the believer's faith in Christ and commitment to follow Him.

We believe the Lord's Supper was instituted by Jesus Christ on the night before His crucifixion and was given to His followers to continue in remembrance of Him.

Through the Lord's Supper, the Church remembers Christ's sacrifice, proclaims His death, looks forward in hope to His return, and expresses fellowship and unity as the one Body of Christ.

Key Scriptures:
Matthew 28:19; Romans 6:3–4; 1 Corinthians 10:16–17; 1 Corinthians 11:23–26
Statement 11

We Believe in the Church

We believe the Church is the Body of Christ, called to proclaim the Gospel, make disciples, worship God, equip believers, and demonstrate His truth and love.

Key Scriptures:
Matthew 28:18–20; 1 Corinthians 12:12; Ephesians 4:11–13
Statement 12

We Believe in Christ's Return

We believe that one day Jesus Christ will personally and gloriously return for His Church. Our hope in His promised return strengthens us to persevere and calls us to live holy, faithful lives devoted to God.

Key Scriptures:
1 Thessalonians 4:16–17; 1 John 3:2–3
Statement 13

We Believe in the Resurrection, Judgment, and Eternity

We believe that believers can have confidence that when they die, their physical bodies remain in the grave while they are present with the Lord. We believe that death is not the end.

Jesus Christ declared that He is the resurrection and the life, and whoever believes in Him, though he dies, yet shall he live.

We believe there will be a bodily resurrection and that God will judge mankind. Scripture teaches that a time is coming when the dead will be raised.

We believe that salvation through Jesus Christ delivers the believer from condemnation. Eternal life begins when a person places his or her faith in Jesus Christ and continues beyond physical death. Believers therefore live with the confident hope of resurrection, eternal life, and everlasting fellowship with the Lord.

Key Scriptures:
Ecclesiastes 3:11; John 3:16; John 5:28–29; John 11:25–26; Romans 8:1; 1 Corinthians 15:42–44, 51–53; 2 Corinthians 5:8; 2 Timothy 1:10

Our Commitment

Hightower Bible Ministry seeks to remain faithful to Scripture, centered upon Jesus Christ, dependent upon the Holy Spirit, and committed to proclaiming the Gospel with truth, grace, and conviction.
