Verse-by-Verse Study

John 2

“Whatever He says to you, do it.” — John 2:5

John 2 reveals Jesus beginning His public ministry through His first sign at Cana and His cleansing of the temple, showing both His grace and His authority.

Chapter Overview

John 2 records two major events: Jesus turning water into wine at a wedding in Cana, and Jesus cleansing the temple in Jerusalem. Together, these events reveal His divine power, His concern for God's glory, and the beginning of His public ministry.

John 2:1–12 — Jesus Turns Water into Wine

Observation Jesus, His mother, and His disciples attend a wedding in Cana. When the wine runs out, Jesus instructs the servants to fill six stone water jars. He then turns the water into wine.
Interpretation This is the first sign recorded in John's Gospel. The miracle reveals Jesus' glory and shows His divine power over creation. It also leads His disciples to believe in Him more deeply.
Application Jesus is able to work in situations that appear ordinary, incomplete, or beyond our ability to fix. Faith responds by trusting His direction and obeying what He says.

John 2:13–25 — Jesus Cleanses the Temple

Observation Jesus enters the temple in Jerusalem and finds merchants and money changers conducting business there. He drives them out and commands them not to turn His Father's house into a marketplace.
Interpretation Jesus demonstrates His authority over the temple and His zeal for the honor of His Father. He also points ahead to His own death and resurrection when He says, “Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up.”
Application Worship must never become empty routine or self-serving activity. Jesus calls us to honor God sincerely and to examine whether our lives reflect reverence, obedience, and devotion to Him.

Key Truths

  • Jesus revealed His glory through His first sign at Cana.
  • Jesus has authority over creation.
  • Faith is strengthened through trusting and obeying Christ.
  • Jesus has authority over true worship.
  • Jesus was deeply concerned for the honor of His Father's house.
  • Jesus pointed ahead to His death and resurrection.

Practical Application

John 2 challenges us to trust Jesus in everyday needs and to take worship seriously. His power is not limited, and His authority deserves obedience. We should ask whether our lives reflect genuine faith, reverence, and readiness to do whatever He says.

Discussion Questions

  1. What does the miracle at Cana reveal about Jesus?
  2. Why is Mary's statement, “Whatever He says to you, do it,” important?
  3. What did the disciples learn from Jesus' first sign?
  4. Why was Jesus angry about what was happening in the temple?
  5. What did Jesus mean when He spoke about raising the temple in three days?
  6. What should John 2 change about the way we trust and worship God?
