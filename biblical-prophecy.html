Welcome to Biblical Prophecy

Hightower Bible Ministry is committed to helping believers and nonbelievers understand biblical prophecy through careful examination of Scripture, without speculation, sensationalism, or fear.

From Genesis to Revelation, God reveals His eternal purpose for mankind. His Word reveals the past, the present, and the future, helping us understand His redemptive plan through Jesus Christ.

Our study of biblical prophecy will remain centered upon Jesus Christ, through whom God's eternal purposes are fulfilled.

Central Truth

Biblical prophecy is not merely a revelation of future events. It reveals God's eternal purpose, centered in Jesus Christ, through whom mankind is redeemed, believers are transformed, and God's people will ultimately dwell with Him forever.

Our Approach to Biblical Prophecy

  • Scripture remains our final authority.
  • Scripture must be understood in its proper context.
  • Jesus Christ remains the central focus.
  • Biblical truth must be distinguished from human speculation and personal opinion.
  • Different prophetic interpretations will be examined carefully in light of Scripture.
  • We will not attempt to establish a date for Christ's return.
  • Biblical prophecy should encourage holiness, faithfulness, evangelism, hope, and spiritual readiness.
Our Teaching Commitment

Each completed lesson will include in-depth biblical commentary, supporting Scriptures, practical Christian application, reflection questions, a unique illustration, and a downloadable PDF for personal or group study.
