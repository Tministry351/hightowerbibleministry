Lesson 4: What Does It Mean to Be Born Again?

Foundational Scripture: John 3:3

John 3:3 (KJV)

“Jesus answered and said unto him, Verily, verily, I say unto thee, Except a man be born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God.”

Welcome — A New Beginning

Have you ever wished you could make a fresh start? In John chapter 3, Jesus explained to a man named Nicodemus that entering God’s kingdom requires something deeper than changing habits or improving our behavior.

Jesus said we must be “born again.” This may sound confusing at first. It does not mean being born physically a second time. Jesus was speaking about the new spiritual life God gives.

1. Why Did Jesus Say We Must Be Born Again?

Nicodemus was a religious teacher who knew much about Scripture. Yet Jesus told him that religious knowledge and position were not enough. Read John 3:1–7.

Every person needs new life from God, whether they grew up in church or have never attended. Being born again is not about joining a particular organization.

Remember: The new birth is a work of God, not a title or achievement we give ourselves.

2. What Does It Mean to Be Born of the Spirit?

Jesus explained that we need to be born of the Spirit (John 3:5–8). The Holy Spirit gives spiritual life and begins changing our hearts.

This new life is not something we can produce simply by trying harder. Titus 3:5 describes salvation as God’s mercy and renewal by the Holy Spirit.

Remember: God brings about the new birth through His Spirit.

3. How Does This Connect to Faith in Jesus?

In the same conversation, Jesus pointed Nicodemus toward faith in the Son of God. John 3:16 teaches that God gave His only begotten Son so that whoever believes in Him should not perish but have everlasting life.

Being born again is not a special experience reserved for a few people. Jesus calls us to believe in Him. The new birth and faith in Christ belong together; they are not two different ways of being saved.

Remember: Our hope rests in Jesus Christ and God’s saving work, not in an emotional moment or a religious ceremony.

4. What Changes When Someone Is Born Again?

Second Corinthians 5:17 says that anyone in Christ is a new creation. This does not mean we become perfect overnight.

As we learn to follow Jesus, the Holy Spirit helps us turn away from sin, grow in love, develop new desires, and obey God’s Word (Galatians 5:22–23). Growth happens over time.

Remember: A changed life is the fruit of God’s work within us, not a way to earn His love.

5. How Can I Begin Growing in This New Life?

Start by reading John 3 slowly. Talk honestly to God in prayer, even if you are unsure how to pray. Find a Bible-believing church where Scripture is taught and people can encourage you.

Continue learning about Jesus through the Bible. Ask questions, seek guidance, and remember that Christian growth is a lifelong journey.

Remember: You do not have to understand everything at once. Keep seeking to know Jesus Christ through His Word.

Something to Think About

  1. What did Jesus tell Nicodemus in John 3:3?
  2. Why is being born again more than making better choices?
  3. What does John 3:16 teach about God’s gift?
  4. What questions do I have about faith and the Holy Spirit?

Take your time with these questions. You are welcome to continue reading Scripture and asking questions as you learn.
