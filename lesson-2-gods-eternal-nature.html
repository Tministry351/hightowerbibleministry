God's Eternal and Self-Existent Nature

Primary Scriptures: Exodus 3:13–15 and Psalm 90:1–2

Lesson objective: Understand what Scripture reveals about God's eternal existence and consider how this truth strengthens our trust in Him and our relationship with Jesus Christ.

1. Observation — What does Scripture say?

Exodus 3:13–15

God called Moses to lead the Israelites out of Egypt. Moses asked what he should say if the Israelites asked for the name of the God who sent him.

“I AM WHO I AM.” — Exodus 3:14 (ESV)

God also identified Himself as the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

What do we observe?

  • God reveals Himself to Moses.
  • God identifies Himself as “I AM.”
  • God connects His identity to His covenant relationship with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.
  • Moses is sent by the God who has been faithful throughout Israel's history.

Psalm 90:1–2

“From everlasting to everlasting you are God.” — Psalm 90:2 (ESV)

God existed before the mountains were formed and before the earth came into existence.

2. Interpretation — What does Scripture mean?

God is eternal

God has no beginning and no end. Everything in the created world has a beginning, but God existed before creation.

God is self-existent

God does not receive His existence from anyone else. He does not depend on anything outside Himself for His life or being.

Within Exodus 3, the statement “I AM WHO I AM” emphasizes God's identity and independent existence while also assuring Moses of His continuing presence and faithfulness.

Supporting passage: Acts 17:24–25 teaches that God does not need anything from humanity; rather, He gives life and breath to all.

3. Meditation — Take Time to Reflect

We experience beginnings, aging, uncertainty, and change. God has neither beginning nor end. Moses could not rely ultimately upon his own ability before Pharaoh; his confidence needed to rest in the God who sent him.

Consider: When we face uncertainty, we can find assurance in the God who has always existed and remains faithful.

4. Application — How Should This Change My Life?

What I learn about GodHow I can respond
God has always existed.I can trust Him with my future.
God needs nothing.I worship Him because He is worthy, not because He needs something from me.
God has been faithful across generations.I can remember His faithfulness when I face difficulties.
My earthly life is limited.I can use my time wisely and seek His purposes.

Personal application: We are called to serve faithfully, but the results of ministry do not rest on our strength alone. We can seek God's guidance and depend upon Him as we serve.

Christ-centered connection: Jesus speaks of knowing the only true God and Jesus Christ whom He sent in John 17:3. Studying God's attributes should lead us toward deeper faith, worship, and obedience—not merely more information.
