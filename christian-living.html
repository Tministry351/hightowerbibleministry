Following Christ

An original Hightower Bible Ministry discipleship course designed to help believers understand God's Word, grow in Christ, and live biblical truth in everyday life.

About This Course

Following Christ is being developed as an original Hightower Bible Ministry discipleship course. Each lesson is rooted in Scripture and follows the HBM teaching rhythm: Read it, Understand it, Believe it, and Live it.

Lessons 1-3

  1. Who Is Jesus Christ?
  2. The Gospel and Salvation
  3. Can I Know I Belong to Christ?

These first three lessons introduce the identity of Jesus Christ, the message of salvation, and the believer's assurance in Christ.

View Lessons 1-3 Download PDF

More Lessons Are Being Developed

Future lessons will continue with Bible study, prayer, faith, forgiveness, walking in the Spirit, overcoming temptation, Christian relationships, serving others, and sharing the Gospel.

HBM Teaching Rhythm

Read it • Understand it • Believe it • Live it

God's Word should not only be read but understood; not only understood but believed; and not only believed but lived.
