Living by Faith

Trusting God Beyond What We See

Biblical faith is trusting God because of who He is and what He has revealed in Scripture, even when circumstances are uncertain and feelings change.

Download / Print PDF

Primary Scriptures

Primary Scripture: 2 Corinthians 5:7

Supporting Scriptures: Hebrews 11:1–6 • Romans 10:17 • Proverbs 3:5–6 • Mark 9:23–24
Central Teaching Truth

Biblical faith is trusting God because of who He is and what He has revealed in Scripture, even when circumstances are uncertain and feelings change.

Lesson Objective

Understand biblical faith, distinguish faith from feelings, and practice trusting God's Word through everyday obedience.

Introduction — When the Outcome Is Unclear

A believer may pray about a family problem, a financial concern, or a difficult decision without seeing an immediate answer.

Uncertainty can bring the question, “Does God hear me?”

Biblical faith is not confidence that every outcome will match our wishes. It is confidence in God's character and wisdom, even while we wait.

1. Observation — What Does Scripture Say?

Read Hebrews 11:1–6 and 2 Corinthians 5:7. Answer from the passages before discussing their application.

2. Interpretation — What Does Scripture Mean?

Faith has an object: the living God revealed in Jesus Christ. It is not merely optimism or confidence in our own ability.

Romans 10:17 connects faith with hearing the message concerning Christ. Biblical trust grows as we attend to what God has spoken.

Hebrews 11 describes people who obeyed God without knowing every detail of what would happen.

Faith does not eliminate questions or grief. It leads us to trust God's character and follow His Word while circumstances remain uncertain.

Feelings are real, but they are changeable. Feelings cannot serve as our final authority.

Scripture teaches us to weigh our responses against God's truth rather than treating emotional certainty as proof of His favor.

Teaching Diagram — The Foundation of Faith

GOD'S WORD What God reveals about Himself and His promises
↓

DAILY OBEDIENCE Following Christ even when feelings and outcomes change
Key Truth: God's Word is the foundation; faith is our response; obedience is one visible expression of that trust.

3. Application — How Do We Live God's Word?

Reflection — Faith Is Not Denial

Trusting God does not mean pretending that pain, fear, or unanswered questions do not exist.

In Mark 9:24, a father brought both belief and weakness to Jesus.

Honest prayer and faithful obedience can coexist with uncertainty.
