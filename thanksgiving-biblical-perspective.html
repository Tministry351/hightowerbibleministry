Thanksgiving: More Than Just a Holiday

A Biblical Conversation About Gratitude and Worship

1. A Personal Reflection

When I think about Thanksgiving Day in America, I see families and friends gathering together, enjoying food, watching football, and spending time with one another.

There is nothing wrong with enjoying these blessings. However, I sometimes wonder whether we have become so involved in the activities of Thanksgiving that we forget the importance of giving thanks to Almighty God.

For Christians, thanksgiving should be more than a prayer before a holiday meal. It should be part of our everyday lives.

Psalm 100:4 (KJV)

"Enter into his gates with thanksgiving, and into his courts with praise: be thankful unto him, and bless his name."

2. Giving Thanks in All Circumstances

It is easy to be thankful when life is going well. But our faith is tested when we face difficulties, unanswered prayers, or circumstances we do not understand.

As we mature in our relationship with Jesus Christ, we learn to trust God even when we do not receive what we hoped for.

1 Thessalonians 5:18 (KJV)

"In every thing give thanks: for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you."

Habakkuk 3:17-18 reminds us that even when earthly provisions fail, we can still rejoice in the God of our salvation.

Our gratitude should not depend only upon favorable circumstances, but upon our confidence in God's faithfulness.

3. Remembering Our Spiritual Blessings

We should be thankful for food, family, health, and the material blessings we receive.

But we should never overlook the spiritual blessings we have through Jesus Christ.

Ephesians 1:3 (KJV)

"Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who hath blessed us with all spiritual blessings in heavenly places in Christ:"

Our salvation, forgiveness, reconciliation with God, and hope of eternal life are reasons for thanksgiving regardless of our earthly circumstances.

The greatest blessings are not always the things we can see or possess.

4. Thanksgiving Is an Act of Worship

Worshiping God should involve every part of our lives.

Romans 12:1 calls believers to present their bodies as living sacrifices to God.

Colossians 3:17 (KJV)

"And whatsoever ye do in word or deed, do all in the name of the Lord Jesus, giving thanks to God and the Father by him."

Whether we are at church, at home, working, or spending time with family, our lives should reflect our gratitude toward God.

Thanksgiving is more than something we say. It should be reflected in how we live.

5. A Final Reflection

Thanksgiving Day comes once a year, but giving thanks to God should be a daily expression of our faith.

We may not always receive what we ask for, and life may not always be easy.

Nevertheless, we can continue trusting, worshiping, and glorifying Almighty God.

The question is not simply whether we celebrate Thanksgiving Day, but whether we live each day with a thankful heart toward God.

1 Corinthians 10:31 (KJV)

"Whether therefore ye eat, or drink, or whatsoever ye do, do all to the glory of God."
