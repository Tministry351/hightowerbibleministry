A Simple Growth Path

  • Know what God's Word says.
  • Understand what it means.
  • Believe what God has revealed.
  • Apply biblical truth to everyday life.
  • Continue growing through prayer, obedience, fellowship, and service.

HBM Teaching Rhythm
