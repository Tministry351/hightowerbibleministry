Studying Genesis More Deeply

Genesis lays the foundation for the entire biblical story. These in-depth studies move beyond a general book overview and examine individual chapters more carefully.

Each study is designed to help you see what the text says, understand what it means, recognize the truths it teaches about God and humanity, and apply those truths faithfully.

Genesis 1–11 — The Beginnings of the World and Humanity

These chapters introduce creation, humanity, sin, judgment, grace, the Flood, the nations, and Babel.

Study God as Creator, the order and goodness of creation, humanity made in God's image, and the foundation Genesis 1 establishes for the rest of Scripture.
